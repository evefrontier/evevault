# Security Policy

This page explains **how to report vulnerabilities privately** in EVE Vault and **what you can expect from us**.

## How to Report
- **Please DO NOT open a public issue for security problems.**
  Email **security@fenris.com** with the subject line:
  `Vulnerability Report: <brief description>`

Include as much detail as you can:
- Commit hash or release tag
- Steps to reproduce (from a clean clone)
- Impact (what an attacker could achieve)
- Your contact info and whether you prefer to remain anonymous

If you're unsure whether something qualifies as a security issue, **send it anyway** and we'll help triage. We aim to acknowledge reports within a few business days and will coordinate disclosure timing with you.

### AI-generated reports
We **do not** accept vulnerability reports that appear to be unverified AI output or generic "potential" issues without a working reproduction. AI tools are fine for analysis, but the report itself must come from a human who has verified the issue, can reproduce it from a clean clone, and can discuss it. Reports that fail this bar will be closed without detailed triage.

## Contact
- **Primary:** security@fenris.com
- **Back-up (non-sensitive questions):** open an Issue in the repo

Thank you for helping keep EVE Vault safe.
